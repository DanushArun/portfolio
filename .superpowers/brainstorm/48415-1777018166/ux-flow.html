Design Proposal: UX Flow & Architecture

Here is how we translate "The Cinematic R&D Lab" with "Lab Departments" into a concrete website structure. Does this high-level flow sound right?

1. The Loading Sequence (The Airlock)

A minimal, cinematic loader. A terminal-style prompt boots up ("Initializing AI Agents...", "Calibrating Physics Engine...") followed by a smooth fade into the main environment. Sets the technical tone immediately.

2. The Hero (The Observatory)

Dark mode. Massive typography: "DANUSH ARUN". Subtitle: "Bridging scientific research and production software." Background features a subtle, slow-rotating WebGL element (like an abstract glass-morphic particle sphere). Scroll hint prompts horizontal movement.

3. Horizontal Lab Corridors (Lenis + GSAP)

Scrolling down actually moves the camera horizontally through the space. Each "Department" (Agentic Core, Physics Lab, EV Control Center) is a distinct visual zone with glassmorphic cards revealing project details (DriveX Mira, Formula Manipal) on hover.

4. The Archive (GitHub & Resume)

A dedicated section seamlessly integrating your open-source work and formal resume details. Presented as an interactive "Data Archive" UI with clean typography rather than a standard PDF link.
