Structuring the Lab

Awesome choice. The Cinematic R&D Lab gives us that Orano Group premium, horizontal-scrolling elegance. Massive typography, dark mode, smooth GSAP animations.

How should we organize your work within this "Lab" environment?

A

The Evolution Timeline

Structure: A chronological journey. Scrolling horizontally takes the user through time, from early data science internships, into the Formula EV leadership, and finally arriving at your current cutting-edge Agentic AI and Physics engine work.

  • Shows clear growth and increasing responsibility.
  • Easy for recruiters to follow your career path.
B

The "Lab Departments"

Structure: Thematic grouping. The screen is divided into conceptual "chambers" or "departments" that the user glides between: The Agentic Core (DriveX), The Physics Simulation Lab (Research), and The Control Center (Formula Manipal).

  • Highly immersive and leans heavily into the "Lab" metaphor.
  • Groups skills logically rather than strictly by time.
C

Impact-First Showcase

Structure: We hit them immediately with your biggest technical achievements (Mira Agentic AI, the Vectorized Physics Engine) as massive, full-screen interactive case studies, followed by a condensed timeline for the rest.

  • Maximum initial impact for an Awwwards audience.
  • Gets right to the best stuff without making them scroll.
