Design Spec: The Event Horizon

I completely get the vibe. It's that visceral, mathematical pull into the unknown. Here is the complete design specification for the portfolio.

Review this carefully. If you approve, I'll write the formal spec document and we'll move to implementation planning.

1. The Transition (The Airlock)

The Scene: The site loads into pitch black. A hyper-realistic, wireframe-style black hole (accretion disk and event horizon) renders using Three.js/WebGL.
The Physics: It uses custom GLSL shaders simulating gravitational lensing and photon rings around a singularity.
The Action: Instead of a "Click to Enter" button, the user scrolls. As they scroll, the camera pushes forward, accelerating into the black hole. The FOV stretches, the wireframes warp dramatically (spaghettification), and the screen goes completely white/black at the singularity point, instantly snapping into the main Lab environment.

2. The Main Experience (The Lab Corridors)

The HUD: You emerge inside "The Lab". It's a horizontal-scrolling experience (Lenis + GSAP). The background remains a mathematically precise, Orano-style wireframe grid that reacts to your scroll velocity (inertia) and cursor mass.
The Content: Massive typography and frosted glassmorphic panels float over the wireframes. The panels contain your "Departments" (Agentic AI, Physics Engine, Formula EV).

3. Tech Stack & Architecture

Framework: Next.js 14 (App Router), React, TypeScript.
3D & Physics: Three.js, React Three Fiber (R3F), Custom GLSL Shaders (for gravitational lensing).
Animation & Scroll: GSAP (ScrollTrigger), Lenis (smooth horizontal scrolling).
Styling: Tailwind CSS (for the glassmorphic HUD overlays and typography).

4. The Archive (Resume & GitHub)

At the end of the horizontal corridor is "The Archive". It pulls live data (or visually simulates it) from your DriveX and personal GitHubs, presented alongside a clean, interactive breakdown of your resume (Education, Skills, Certifications) mapped to the wireframe grid.
