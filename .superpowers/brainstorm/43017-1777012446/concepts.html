The Core Metaphor

You have a deeply technical background (Agentic AI, Computational Physics, Formula EV) but want a "human, non-generic" feel. Your inspirations (Bruno Simon, Orano, CartoonEast) all share one thing: immersion.

Which of these storylines feels most like "you"?

A

The Physics & Motion Playground

The Vibe: Bruno Simon meets Formula Manipal. A playful, fully interactive 3D WebGL environment. Instead of scrolling a page, the user "drives" or navigates a small autonomous entity (like your FM23e) through a stylized world representing your career.

  • Highly interactive, immediate "wow" factor for Awwwards.
  • Showcases your physics engine & autonomous path planning skills literally.
B

The Cinematic R&D Lab

The Vibe: Orano Group meets FuryX. A sleek, horizontal-scrolling masterpiece using Lenis and GSAP. Dark mode, massive elegant typography, and "glassmorphic" layers. 3D elements are abstract and cinematic (e.g., a rotating computational physics model, data nodes connecting).

  • Extremely premium, serious, and professional.
  • Focuses on beautiful typography, smooth easing, and high-end aesthetics.
C

The Narrative Bridge (Human & Technical)

The Vibe: CartoonEast's storytelling combined with sleek tech. A linear story about "Bridging scientific research and production software." As the user scrolls, interactive 3D vignettes assemble on screen (a PCB testing rig, a quantum circuit, an AI agent dashboard) before dissolving into the next chapter.

  • Puts your human story and mission front-and-center.
  • Allows for pacing and editorial control, highly "Awwwards" friendly storytelling.
