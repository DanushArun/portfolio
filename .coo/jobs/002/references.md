# Awwwards references — portfolio direction (Job 002)

Date: 2026-05-12
Researcher: deep-researcher (via general-purpose fallback)
Criteria: Awwwards SOTM / SOTY / SOTD / Honors, last ~18 months, sharing territory with a 3D founder portfolio with scrollytelling narrative.

## TL;DR
- Strongest winners converge on strict two-color palettes (one dark + one cream/grey accent) and treat motion as the *primary* communication channel, not decoration.
- "Cinematic" on Awwwards 2024–2026 means deliberate pacing, scroll-scrubbed beats, restrained type that goes BIG only at narrative pivots, and ONE signature interaction that demonstrates fluency without becoming a gimmick.
- Generic "particles everywhere" WebGL is out; thematic 3D where the geometry *means something* (helmet, planet, sculpture) is in.

## References

### [1] Lando Norris — Site of the Year 2025
- **URL:** https://landonorris.com
- **Awwwards status:** Site of the Year 2025, agency OFF+BRAND. Jury 43/253.
- **Why it won:** "Every pixel feels like it is moving at race pace." One hero 3D object (rotating helmet) carries the page; WebGL + Rive hybrid keeps perf tight.
- **Territory overlap:** Personal-brand portfolio (one-person narrative site) using 3D as protagonist.
- **Specific principle to STEAL:** One signature 3D object as recurring protagonist. Don't scatter 3D — make ONE hero geometry (for us: the black hole) reappear at narrative pivots framed differently each time (close-up, wide, silhouette). Treat it like a character with on-screen blocking.
- **What NOT to steal:** Their papaya/lime accent — works for motorsport, looks derivative on a cosmic portfolio.

### [2] Igloo Inc — SOTD Jul 2024 / SOTY 2024 contender
- **URL:** https://igloo.inc
- **Awwwards status:** SOTD Jul 23, 2024 (7.92/10), animations 9.60/10.
- **Why it won:** Two-color palette (#b6bac5 + #383e4e) plus extreme motion craft — restraint plus motion beats ornate visuals.
- **Territory overlap:** Dark, narrative-led WebGL site with palette discipline.
- **Specific principle to STEAL:** Section transitions as *scene cuts*. Each section change uses a motion cut (mask wipe, color flip, camera dolly) instead of soft fade. Film-edit feel — exactly what an 18-stage journey needs.
- **What NOT to steal:** Grey-on-grey runs cold; cosmic narrative needs a warm anchor (ember, sodium) to keep humans in frame.

### [3] David Whyte Experience — SOTD Dec 2024
- **URL:** https://davidwhyte.com/experience
- **Awwwards status:** Site of the Day Dec 31, 2024 (7.63/10). Creativity 8.01.
- **Why it won:** Immersive journey through watercolor landscapes for a poet. Two colors (#E1E1E1 + #000000), gesture-revealed content, scroll-driven landscape transitions.
- **Territory overlap:** Literary single-author narrative — same archetype as founder portfolio.
- **Specific principle to STEAL:** Full-screen single-statement panels between scenes. Between heavy 3D phases, drop into a quiet full-screen panel with ONE sentence in restrained type — a breath. Cinematic "title card" that makes cosmic chapters hit harder.
- **What NOT to steal:** Watercolor-on-cursor effect — on-theme for a poet, decorative for a founder.

### [4] Lusion v3 — SOTD Oct 2023 (studio-portfolio benchmark)
- **URL:** https://lusion.co
- **Awwwards status:** SOTD Oct 2, 2023 (8.25/10) + Dev Award. Perfect 10/10 animations.
- **Why it won:** Scroll-scrubbed 3D that surfaces ongoing experiments *alongside* finished work. Two-color (#1a2ffb + #f0f1fa).
- **Territory overlap:** Studio/founder portfolio that doubles as a tech demo.
- **Specific principle to STEAL:** Show *process* alongside *outcome*. Inside one work phase, embed a raw experiment / WIP shader / failed prototype as a live interactive. This is what separates founder portfolio from CV — credibility through artefacts of thinking.
- **What NOT to steal:** Cursor-magnet on every link gets tiring at length. Use cursor-reactive sparingly.

### [5] Immersive Garden — SOTD Jan 2025
- **URL:** https://immersive-g.com
- **Awwwards status:** Site of the Day Jan 7, 2025 (8.0/10). Animations 8.8/10.
- **Why it won:** "Decade of innovation" portfolio with bas-relief WebGL, sophisticated menu transitions. Black + grey only.
- **Territory overlap:** Dark, type-forward, WebGL-driven portfolio of a single creative entity.
- **Specific principle to STEAL:** Bas-relief / depth-shifted hover on project tiles — image lifts off the page via parallax + subtle shadow + scale. Achievable with R3F on a plane + height map. Dimensional weight without committing to a full 3D scene per tile.
- **What NOT to steal:** "Rapid scroll" / hijacked velocity. Keep native scroll; let ScrollTrigger do scrubbed pinning.

### [6] Zentry — SOTD Aug 2024
- **URL:** https://zentry.com
- **Awwwards status:** SOTD Aug 2024 (7.67/10). Creativity 8.09, dev motion 8.20.
- **Why it won:** Pure #000000, audio-visual storytelling, scroll-driven narrative as content-delivery.
- **Territory overlap:** Black-background narrative site that uses scroll as the primary verb.
- **Specific principle to STEAL:** Audio as a *gated layer*, not default. Muted by default with explicit "Sound on" prompt at cinematic phase opener. Ambient drone for cosmic phases, kill for work phases.
- **What NOT to steal:** Pure unrelieved black. Add atmospheric tint variation per phase — cosmic = black-to-violet, work = black-to-ember — so the journey *feels* like moving through different rooms.

### [7] Siena Film Foundation — SOTD Mar 2025
- **URL:** https://siena.film
- **Awwwards status:** SOTD Mar 18, 2025 (7.9/10). Animations 8.6/10.
- **Why it won:** Cinematic two-color (#000000 + #FAF7EF cream), brand intro animation, film-strip slider.
- **Territory overlap:** Cinema-themed restrained type, dark+cream palette, narrative intro.

### [8] Cartier Watches & Wonders 2025 — SOTD Aug 2025
- **URL:** https://cartier-waw-0225.dev.60fps.fr
- **Awwwards status:** SOTD Aug 18, 2025 (7.64/10). Animations 9.0/10. Immersive Garden + 60fps + mooders sound design.
- **Why it won:** "3D Universes" stitched as cinematic chapters with bespoke sound design and 9.0/10 motion.
- **Territory overlap:** Multi-chapter 3D narrative — analogous to 9-cosmic + 9-work structure.

### [9] Dark Star Labs — Honorable Mention Apr 2026
- **URL:** https://darkstarlabs.io
- **Awwwards status:** Honorable Mention Apr 11, 2026. Avg 7.5/10, two 9.9/10 scores.
- **Why it won:** Space-themed cinematic storytelling. Orange-on-dark duotone, terminal-style loading ("Uploading dsl solutions… > Connecting to DSL database…"), hero's-journey flow.
- **Territory overlap:** Direct — cosmic/space narrative + dark + duotone + chapters.

### [10] Active Theory V6 — SOTD Feb 2024
- **URL:** https://activetheory.net
- **Awwwards status:** SOTD Feb 6, 2024 (7.95/10) + Dev Award. Animations 9.60/10.
- **Why it won:** "New rendering techniques, AI chat, multiplayer" — invisible-tech in service of immersion.
- **Territory overlap:** Tech-forward studio portfolio that *is* the tech demo.
- **Specific principle to STEAL:** "Rendering technique credit line" in footer/about — name the shaders and techniques (SDF, raymarched accretion disk, GPU instancing) in dry technical voice. Code-level credibility signaling that lands with the developer jury.
- **What NOT to steal:** Studio-roster sub-pages — a solo founder has no team to list; reusing the pattern exposes the absence.

## Cross-cutting patterns observed
- **Two-color discipline.** 9 of 10 refs use strict 2-color palettes (one dark + cream/grey/accent). Discipline reads as taste.
- **Motion is the highest-scored axis.** Every ref scored animations 8.0+; SOTY winner won *on motion craft*. Motion budget should exceed visual-design budget.
- **Two-color + scroll-scrubbed pinning + one signature 3D object** is the modal Awwwards-winner recipe.
- **Type goes BIG only at narrative pivots.** Body restrained; display type appears only at chapter beats.
- **Sound, when present, is gated and per-chapter.** Default-muted, explicit opt-in, ambient layers swap on phase change.

## Anti-patterns observed in winners
- **Scroll-hijacking via custom velocity.** Use native scroll + ScrollTrigger pin/scrub.
- **Cursor-trail particle effects.** 2018 novelty; absent from any 2025+ winner above.
- **WebGL-just-because backgrounds.** Every 3D element in winners *is* the content or *advances* the narrative.
