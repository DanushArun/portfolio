## Spec
The C03 → C04 → C05 transition currently visually retreats away from the black hole at the moment the founder expects the camera to *enter* the dark central void. Founder feedback verbatim: "I want the user to fall into the black space in between. Not stretch back outwards as it does now... right now, it goes nearby, stretches, and then goes back again, and then the black hole becomes smaller. That does not give the feeling of going into the black hole, rather the opposite."

Root cause confirmed via read-only investigation:
- Imperative BH canvas (`src/lib/blackHole/index.ts`) drives camera correctly from `(0, 0, -2)` (line 646, "Entering the Event Horizon") through Phase C tunnel `camZ = -2 - 12*u` (line 660) to `camZ = -73` (line 691).
- At `phase === 'C05_WARP'`, `src/components/scene/SceneManager.tsx:70-73` enables the R3F Canvas alongside the BH canvas and crossfades via `bhAlpha`.
- `src/components/scene/CameraRig.tsx:16-42` has no handler for `C05_WARP` — falls through to idle pose `(0, 2, 30)` at line 43-46.
- Result: viewer sees BH camera at `(0, 0, -2)` fade out while R3F camera at `(0, 0, 30)` fades in. 103-unit world-space discontinuity. Eye reads it as the camera retreating outward.

The fix: keep C05_WARP entirely in the imperative BH canvas. The R3F Canvas joins at C06_ANOMALY where its own scene content actually exists. The wormhole tunnel already lives in the imperative renderer; no scene content lost.

## Decisions

### 2026-05-12 — Option A locked
Per Explore investigation report, two fixes were possible:
- **A. Keep C05 entirely in the imperative BH canvas** (chosen).
- **B. Make CameraRig handle C05 by mirroring the BH camera math.**

Rationale: A is one-file, low-risk, removes the entire class of "two cameras disagree" bugs. C05 has no R3F-specific scene content today — the wormhole tunnel and streaks already render in the imperative canvas (`src/lib/blackHole/index.ts:660-697`). DOM text overlays for C05's script copy can render above the imperative canvas without R3F. B would have introduced math-drift risk and required carrying two parallel cameras forever.

## Risks / open questions
- **Risk**: R3F mounts later in scroll than before. If `<Canvas>` first-mount cost is noticeable, the C05 → C06 transition could feel laggy. Mitigation: 3d-graphics-engineer pre-mounts the R3F Canvas off-screen during C05 (display:none / opacity:0) so its assets / shaders are warm by the time C06 makes it visible.
- **Open**: HUD currently shows `01 / 18` not `01 / 23`. Confirms the audit's expectation — `src/lib/journey-map.ts` is still the 18-phase definition. L00 + the 23-phase target arrive in Job 006 and onward. Not part of this bugfix.
